Question 1: Choose the word whose underlined part is pronounced differently:
A. cat
B. hat
*C. car
D. fat
Explanation: "car" contains the long vowel /ɑː/, whereas "cat", "hat", and "fat" contain /æ/.

Question 2: She _____ to school by bus every morning.
*A. goes
B. go
C. going
D. went
Explanation: "every morning" indicates Present Simple tense with third-person singular subject "She" -> goes.

Question 3: Plants use sunlight, water, and carbon dioxide to create oxygen and energy through _____.
A. evaporation
*B. photosynthesis
C. gravity
D. distillation
Explanation: Photosynthesis is the biological process by which green plants synthesize nutrients using sunlight.

Question 4: If it _____ sunny tomorrow, our class will visit the science museum.
A. will be
B. were
*C. is
D. had been
Explanation: First conditional structure: If + S + V(present simple), S + will + V-infinitive.
